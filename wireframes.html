01

Colour as direction, not as decoration

The site sits on a near-white ground everywhere. A direction's colour appears only when you are inside that direction: as the section marker, the active nav underline, headings' accent, buttons and chart fills. Home passes through all four as you scroll, but never shows more than one at a time.

Blue
საზოგადოება

Meetups, gamejams, masterclasses, socials, the Telegram, everything that gathers people.

Red
გამომცემლობა

Publishing: strategy, business, Steam launch, mentorship, genre and market analysis, creator and publisher databases.

Green
აქსელერაცია

Incubator, accelerator, cohorts, funding, relocation, the large multi-year programmes.

Yellow
მონაცემები

Industry data and insights: the Georgian games database, studio and market stats, yearly reports.

04

Responsive rules

Fixing the responsive problem means deciding these once and applying them everywhere, instead of per page.

Grid

One fluid container, max-width 1180px, side padding that grows with the viewport. All card grids are auto-fit / minmax — they reflow at any width instead of at three fixed breakpoints.

Type

Headings use clamp() so nothing needs a mobile override. Body never below 16px. Georgian and Latin share one scale — Noto Sans Georgian for both.

Touch

Every tap target at least 44px. Filter chips become a bottom sheet, not a cramped row. Long rails become snap-scrollers with visible overflow.

Motion

Scroll-driven accent changes and the drifting logo are decorative — all disabled under prefers-reduced-motion and on low-power devices. The mini game never autoplays.

Images

Two ratios only: 460×215 for game capsules, 1:1 for logos and avatars. Missing art renders as a neutral block with the name set in it — never a broken image.

Language

Georgian is the default; the EN toggle stays in the header on every breakpoint. All record names, descriptions and statuses are bilingual in the data, as they already are.

06

Decisions from your review

Settled
  • Photos stay as placeholders you swap by dropping a file in one folder — one line per image, nothing else to touch
  • Numbers and all studio / game data are yours to fill; the site asks people to submit their own
  • Donations go only to kisa.ge/donate/ggc, with your QR in both colours
  • Partners move off Donate into About
Steam parsing, plainly
  • Steam blocks a browser from reading its pages directly, so "on every load" needs a middleman
  • Plan: a small job inside your own GitHub repo refreshes every game from Steam / itch / App Store / Play a few times a day and stores the result — visitors then always see current data instantly, with no server to pay for and nothing manual
  • The store page itself is still read live where the platform allows it
The mini game — my pick
  • Four petals of the logo orbit a centre; colour-coded targets arrive and you rotate the mark to catch the matching colour
  • One control, three seconds to understand, speeds up fast — difficult, as you asked, and built out of the brand itself
  • The same logo character does the small interaction animations: it folds into a paper plane when a form is sent
